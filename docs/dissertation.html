Help Isn’t Everywhere: Mapping Access to Harm Reduction Services

Status: In Progress · Supported by the College of Arts & Sciences Dissertation Research Fellowship

This dissertation investigates spatial disparities in harm reduction service availability across five U.S. states (California, Florida, Indiana, New York, and Ohio). I examine how state-level policies interact with geographic context to create “service deserts,” with particular attention to the structural barriers faced by rural communities.

Unit of analysis: County-level States: California, Florida, Indiana, New York, Ohio Service types: Syringe Service Programs (SSP), Naloxone Distribution, Supportive/Recovery Housing, Substance Use Treatment

    • New York — extreme urban concentration
    • Ohio — the unexpected leader
